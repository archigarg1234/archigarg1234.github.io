AI Research Engineer

Brainwave Science  ·  India

I am an AI Research Engineer at Brainwave Science, working on EEG-based consumer preference decoding.

I hold a BS-MS in Biology (Data Science minor) from IISER Mohali. My master's thesis investigated the neural coding of anxiety and valence in the mouse anterior insula using machine learning, carried out at the Beyeler Lab, Neurocenter Magendie, Bordeaux, France.

I am broadly interested in how the brain represents subjective states — preference, affect, anxiety — and how we can decode them reliably from neural recordings in real-world settings.
