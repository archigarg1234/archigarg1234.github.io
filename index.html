About

Brain signals,
decoded.

I'm an AI Research Engineer at Brainwave Science, working at the intersection of neural signal processing and machine learning. My work focuses on extracting meaningful cognitive states from consumer-grade EEG devices — low hardware, high science.

My background spans computational neuroscience (Master's thesis on anxiety and valence coding in the mouse anterior insula at Bordeaux) and applied ML across time-series, clinical data, and real-time BCI systems.

I hold a BS-MS in Biology (Data Science Minor) from IISER Mohali, and have two peer-reviewed publications and a COSYNE 2025 poster to my name.
